## Full description
---
Take back control of your Twitter/X feed.

Twitter constantly pushes new posts into your timeline, making it impossible to read without the feed jumping around — or pulling you into an endless scroll. Twitter New Post Blocker stops that.

**What it does**
• Blocks new posts from loading into your feed as you browse
• Hides the "X new posts" banner that tempts you to load more
• Shows a friendly reminder that life exists outside the app

**Scheduling**
Don't want it on all the time? Set a schedule. Pick the hours you want blocking active — like during work, or late at night — and posts load normally outside those hours.

**No account needed. No data collected. No strings attached.**
Everything runs locally in your browser. The extension cannot read your DMs, post on your behalf, or access your account in any way.

**How to use**
1. Install the extension — a one-time setup screen appears
2. Choose: block always, block on a schedule, or keep off
3. That's it. The extension runs silently in the background

Click the extension icon any time to change your settings.

---
Built for people who want to read Twitter intentionally, not reactively.

## GitHub Pages deployment (for privacy policy)

1. Create a new public GitHub repo, e.g. `twitter-post-blocker-privacy`
2. Add `privacy-policy.html` to the root and rename it to `index.html`
3. Go to repo Settings → Pages → set source to `main` branch
4. Your URL will be: `https://<your-username>.github.io/twitter-post-blocker-privacy/`
5. Paste that URL into the Chrome Web Store privacy policy field
