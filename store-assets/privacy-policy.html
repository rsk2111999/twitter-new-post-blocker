Twitter New Post Blocker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Ever.

What data does this extension access?

The extension reads the Twitter/X page you are already viewing in order to detect and hide new posts as they load. It does not read, store, or transmit any content from those posts.

What data does this extension store?

The only data stored is your preferences — whether blocking is on or off, and your optional schedule (start time and end time). This is stored locally in your browser using the standard Chrome storage API (chrome.storage.sync). It is never sent to any external server.

Does this extension communicate with any server?

No. The extension makes zero network requests. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party scripts.

Does this extension use cookies?

No.

Does this extension access my Twitter/X account?

No. The extension cannot log in, log out, post, read your DMs, or take any action on your behalf. It only observes the visible page to detect and hide incoming posts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Given the nature of this extension (no data collection), meaningful changes are unlikely.
